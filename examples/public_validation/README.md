# Public-case input fixtures

These files transcribe only the numerical facts required to exercise the released KinetiCheck
workflow. Publisher PDFs are not redistributed. Provenance, source-file checksums, license notes,
formula audits, and excluded comparisons are recorded in
[`docs/public-data-sources.json`](../../docs/public-data-sources.json) and
[`docs/public-data-validation.md`](../../docs/public-data-validation.md).

- `KC-PUB-001`: methane dehydroaromatization over Mo/HZSM-5, Mears external mass.
- `KC-PUB-002`: acetic-acid hydrodeoxygenation over Mo2C, Weisz-Prater internal mass.
- `KC-PUB-003`: glycerol oxidation over Pt/Cu-CuZrOx, Mears external mass.

The fixtures reproduce published arithmetic; they do not independently validate measured material
properties, transfer correlations, or the authors' conclusion that intrinsic kinetics were proven.
